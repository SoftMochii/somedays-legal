Delete your account

Last updated: September 20, 2026

You can delete your Somedays account and everything stored with it at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent — we cannot restore an account once it has been deleted.

Delete from inside the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect straight away.

  • Open Somedays and go to the You tab.
  • Open Settings.
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email feedback@somedays.app from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your sign-in account and profile.
  • Every collection you created, and the saved places, notes and photos stored in them.
  • Your trips and their day-by-day plans.
  • Your history of links and screenshots submitted for place extraction.

Collections you created are deleted for everyone — if you had shared one, the people you invited will no longer have access to it. Collections that someone else shared with you are not affected by deleting your account; they belong to whoever created them.

Cancel a paid subscription separately. Deleting your account does not cancel an active Somedays Pro subscription, because billing is handled by the app store and not by us. Cancel it in the Google Play Store or in Apple's App Store subscription settings before deleting your account, or you may continue to be charged.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and billing records are held by Google Play or Apple, not by us. We cannot delete them on your behalf, and they are subject to those companies' own retention policies.
  • Product analytics are recorded against a random identifier rather than your name or email, and are retained in aggregate so we can understand how the app is used. These records cannot be tied back to you after your account is gone.
  • Server logs may retain routine technical entries for a short period as part of normal operation, and are overwritten on a rolling basis.

If you have a question about any of this, or want confirmation once a deletion has gone through, contact us at feedback@somedays.app.
