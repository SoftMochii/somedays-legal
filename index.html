Somedays

Turn Instagram Reels and TikToks into mapped travel collections you can plan with.

Stop losing great finds in your saved folder. Somedays pulls the restaurants, cafés, shops and hidden gems out of the videos you save, and turns them into collections you can actually plan a trip with — mapped, rated, and ready to share with friends.

How it works

  1. Share any Instagram Reel or TikTok to Somedays, or paste the link.
  2. We find the places in the video and add them to your collection.
  3. See everything on a map with ratings, photos and opening hours.
  4. Invite friends with a link and plan the trip together.

Available for iPhone and Android. Questions? feedback@somedays.app
